How to read these metrics

RMSSD — resting vagal tone

Root-mean-square of successive RR-interval differences, computed in the pre- and post-session 5-minute resting windows only (RMSSD is only valid at rest -- variable/paced breathing inside the session inflates it mechanically). Higher = more parasympathetic (vagal) activity at rest.

SD1/SD2 — short- vs long-term variability balance

SD1 is fast, beat-to-beat, vagally-linked variability (mathematically close to RMSSD). SD2 is the longer-term/total component -- a mix of both autonomic branches, not a pure sympathetic marker. In the arc this project tracks (SNS dip at Stage 4, PNS rebound at Stage 10), sympathetic activation suppresses the fast component more than the slow one, so SD2 and SD1/SD2 usually move together (both down under stress, both up on recovery) rather than opposing each other. They're most informative when they diverge.

SD2 — autonomic flexibility, in-session

Preferred total-variability marker for the shorter, non-resting windows used inside a session (SD2 tolerates short/non-resting windows better than SDNN -- see hrv_core.py's own design rationale). Used for both the pre/post baseline comparison and the resilience arc.

Resilience Arc = Stage 10 SD2 − Stage 4 SD2

Stage 4 ("Apoptosis") is where the protocol expects an SNS-driven dip; Stage 10 ("Internalization") is where it expects a PNS-driven rebound. A positive arc means SD2 recovered above the Stage-4 low point within the same session.

Recovery % — post- vs. pre-session baseline

Percent change from the pre-session resting baseline to the post-session resting baseline. Positive = the person left the session more relaxed (by that metric) than they arrived.

DFA α1 — fractal complexity

Detrended fluctuation analysis scaling exponent. ~1.0 is read as healthy complexity; well below ~0.75 suggests more random/less organized beat-to-beat structure; well above ~1.4 suggests overly rigid structure. Most informative when it moves opposite to RMSSD.

⚠ Caveat — SD2 window-length consistency under review

SD2 in this report is computed over windows of different lengths: fixed 5-minute pre/post baselines, but also per-stage windows (the resilience arc and its inputs), whose durations vary by stage and by session. SD2 is mathematically derived from SDNN and grows with window length, so comparisons that mix window lengths -- including the resilience arc and the SD2 recovery % below -- may partly reflect that duration difference rather than a genuine physiological change. This is being reviewed; treat those two figures as provisional until SD2 is recomputed on a single, fixed window throughout.
