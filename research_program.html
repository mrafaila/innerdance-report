What we've learned so far

Clinic patients need their own lane — a clearly depressed HRV pattern showed up for the clinic patient, separate from the general population.

Data quality gate is now a priority — robust artefact removal is being added to the pipeline before we trust any reading.

Not ready to name states yet — "Deep Recovery", "Flow", "Focus" are useful labels, but the HRV rules behind them aren't defined with confidence.

Sequence going forward: define reportable participant states first, then correlate to documented HRV metrics — not the other way round.

Some states (anxiety, emotional release, insight) may need more than HRV — open question on what else to collect.

Phase 2 · proposed

Next steps

Decide the objective state and the initial participant group.

Formulate a hypothesis, grounded in existing research and its potential impact.

Extend the hypothesis with a causal model — which acoustic features drive which effect.

Define and select playlists with strong enough differentiation on those features.

Define the data collection protocol, questionnaires, and grouping strategy.

Record and analyze iteratively — a pilot study designed to be publishable.

A first hypothesis on the table

A weekly stress/recovery protocol, after screening, measurably and subjectively improves four areas:

01

Flow state

Calm hyper-vigilance — sharp and energized, not frantic.

Track: HR up with stable/rising RMSSD; Flow State Scale
02

Cardiovascular stability

The parasympathetic "brake" stays active while the "gas" is on.

Track: resting HR ↓, morning RMSSD ↑ over weeks
03

Rapid recovery

Doesn't stay stuck in fight-or-flight after a stressor.

Track: Heart Rate Recovery, first 1–2 min post-stress
04

Sleep & autonomic reset

Evening downregulation clears daytime stress hormones.

Track: waking RMSSD trend; Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index

Open questions

  • Which population should we focus on first — and are effects clearer in healthy, first-time participants?
  • Do we track how well HRV reflects felt improvement, or simply how much HRV improves?
  • Which outcome states are both self-reportable and already correlated with HRV?
  • Can "insight" signals be detected at all from physiology alone?
  • Do we also collect next-day data, not just immediate pre/post?
