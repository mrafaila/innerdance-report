Question 2

Which stages map most cleanly to coactivation, and what that means in plain terms

Coactivation (Weissman & Mendes 2021): sympathetic and parasympathetic activity both elevated relative to baseline, specifically during post-stressor recovery. Their measures (RSA, PEP) require impedance cardiography; nothing below is that measurement. This is a self-report proxy: min(Activation, Calm) computed per response, then averaged — Distress reported alongside, never subtracted. — Source: coactivation-weissman-mendes-2021.md, coactivation-proxy-analysis.md.

Cleanest well-sampled mapping: Stage 7 (Illumination), N=30.

Highest Calm (4.14) and highest proxy (3.68) among stages with N≥20, with Distress unremarkable (2.73). Translation to reported experience: the stage's top-rated items are "delighted" (4.20, n=10), "happy" (4.17, n=6), and "calm" (4.08, n=12) — i.e., respondents most commonly reported feeling delighted/happy and calm together, not one or the other.

Source: coactivation-proxy-analysis.md; track-inquiry-survey-aggregate.md (top-3 items table)
Cleanest mapping overall, but thin sample: Stage 12 (Re-integration), N=9.

Highest proxy (4.32) and lowest Distress (1.47) of any stage. Top items: "inspired" (5.00, n=7), "delighted" (4.67, n=6), "relaxed" (4.00, n=5). N=9 is below the ~30 this dataset treats as stable elsewhere — stated as a lead, not a confirmed result.
